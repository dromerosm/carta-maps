Privacy & data

Carta turns city maps into prints. You do not need an account. We do not add advertising, analytics, tracking cookies or device fingerprinting. Your print is assembled and exported in your browser.

Last updated: 15 September 2026. This notice covers Carta at carta.example.com.

Who is responsible

You can contact him through his published LinkedIn profile, including about privacy or your rights. LinkedIn is a separate service with its own privacy terms; do not send sensitive information in a public post.

Storage and retention

  • Shared search cache: place results are stored for seven days in Cloudflare Durable Objects, under a hash of the normalized query and language. They are shared across visitors, without a user account or IP address attached. The cache is deleted by an expiry alarm. Cloudflare manages any infrastructure backups under its service terms.
  • Map cache: tile metadata is cached for one hour. Tiles from a dated OpenFreeMap release can be cached for up to one year in Cloudflare and your browser. Generic missing-tile responses are cached at Cloudflare for 30 seconds to reduce repeated provider requests; browsers are told not to store those errors. Tile cache entries contain map data, not a search history linked to you.
  • Browser storage: IndexedDB stores recent city results and street geometry. Entries are reused for up to seven days and pruned on later reads or writes; a closed browser cannot run that cleanup. The cache is limited to roughly 40 entries. The theme preference in localStorage remains until you clear it. These stores belong to your browser profile and may be accessible to someone using the same profile.
  • Abuse controls: daily hashes derived from your network IP are used for short rate counters at Cloudflare. Separate counters limit city searches, map requests and requests that need to fetch map data from the provider. People sharing a network share these allowances. The application does not attach raw IP addresses to search or map cache entries, or forward them to the map providers. Cloudflare still processes network information to deliver and secure the service.
  • Application logs: automatic invocation logs and tracing are disabled in this configuration. The application can record a generic failure code, without search text, print content or IP addresses. Cloudflare may retain separate operational or security information under its policies.

Use Clear saved data in the editor to clear Carta’s IndexedDB cache and theme preference on this device. Your browser’s site settings can also clear its HTTP cache and other site data. Clearing the editor does not delete files you already downloaded or purge shared provider caches.

Why we process this information

We rely on legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR to deliver the city-print tool you request, keep it reliable, prevent abuse and respect provider quotas. Processing is limited to what those purposes need. If you contact us, we use the information you provide to respond and handle the request. We keep correspondence only as long as needed to resolve it and meet any applicable legal obligations.

Carta’s own configuration contains no optional analytics or marketing storage. Storage serves the requested editor, remembered appearance and repeated map loads. You can use the editor when persistent browser storage is unavailable; searches and maps may need to be fetched again.

Providers and international processing

Cloudflare supplies hosting, caching, security and the shared search service. The OpenStreetMap Foundation supplies Nominatim search results. OpenFreeMap supplies map geometry. These providers may process information through infrastructure outside Spain or the European Economic Area. Their terms explain their processing, retention and applicable transfer safeguards; Carta does not promise that every request stays in the EU.

Your rights

Where the GDPR applies, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, and portability where applicable, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact Diego Romero using the contact route above and describe your request. We may need enough information to verify and locate the relevant data; anonymous map-cache entries may not be attributable to a particular visitor. We do not collect additional identity information just to identify cache users.

You may complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) or your local supervisory authority. Carta does not make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects, and does not build advertising profiles.
